HOW I
BUILT IT

This log is my process record. The game is built in small steps: each one is a short work session that ends with a commit, a saved change in version control on GitHub. Between some steps I play-test the game and decide what to fix next (each round is an iteration).

It reads every commit straight from GitHub, so it’s always up to date, and old versions stay playable so you can see how the game grew. All the code is open: see how it works, or fork it and make your own version →

New here? Start with the project and my guiding question. The tools are on the Research page.

FEEDBACK & REVISIONS

What each play-test found, what changed because of it, and why. All the feedback so far comes from my own play-tests. Not every idea was used: some things were tested and kept as they were.

PLAY-TESTFEEDBACKREVISION OR DECISIONWHY
CP-AOct 18 Plain Canvas needed about 150 lines of setup and nothing felt heavy. One fixed player size looked right at the back of the court but like a giant at the front wall. Stayed with plain Canvas and dropped Phaser. Kept the camera. Planned three player sizes.Play S1 → Phaser was only the fallback if the test went badly. True perspective would halve the player’s height from back to front, so the sizes split the difference.
CP-BOct 19 View readable, pace right. Dives came at the right moments, but 2.5 s on the ground was too long. The serve should land in different spots depending on where the server stands, and at different depths for HARD and SOFT. The player’s size range was right, but the growth didn’t look natural. S4b: an aimed serve, a 2 s dive and one smooth height curve. Kept as they were: the planted swing, and a press before the ball reaches the front wall still being a miss.Play S4b → The plan offered a way to shuffle during a swing. It wasn’t needed, because the planted swing felt fine.
CP-COct 20 Lost 4–9 to the level 1 CPU. Levels 1, 5 and 9 felt about the same. SOFT in the front court ran too deep instead of dropping short. The CPU did nothing silly. S6b: a slower CPU at the low levels, shot quality that grows with the level, and short front-court drops. The game’s pace by level stayed the same.Play S6b → Level 1 has to be beatable. Only the pace and the reaction time changed between levels, and that wasn’t enough to feel. The drops depart from the arcade’s tables on purpose.
CP-DOct 21 The racket’s height at contact needed fixing. The serve needed a wind-up so its animation shows. The overhead swing should be used. “I want to make the court a little wider.” S8b: a small racket shift by ball height instead of new art, a 7-frame serve wind-up, the overhead swing, and a wider camera, picked from two options and then frozen.Play S8b → The camera had to be frozen before the court was painted to match it.
CP-EOct 22 Text readable, ball always visible, HUD not distracting, and no art needed for MISS or pause. The racket should look more like it hit the ball. S12: a small sideways racket shift. Tried several settings in the running game and picked 1% up or down and 1% across.Play S12 → At that setting the player stays on their spot, at most 1 px off, and the racket stays near where the art holds it.
CP-FOct 22, first pass The hitting range should be more realistic: less reach behind, a slightly wider backhand and a slightly shorter forehand. S12c: reach set by direction, in the shape of an oval. The oval means the far corners can’t be hit.
CP-FOct 22, replay The range should be “more like a rectangle with curved corners”. The school’s shield should go in the match. S12d: a squarer range (a superellipse), and the shield in the match’s margin. The corner shape was picked by playing four options, not only from the map.
CP-FOct 23, second replay The words on the front wall should stay up longer, and the wall should say when a close game needs more points. S12e: callouts of about 1.5 s each, and a FIRST TO notice. Win by 2 can raise the score needed to win, so the wall now says when it does.
CP-FOct 23, final One 1-player and one 2-player match from the title screen, at least one past 10–10. Nothing to fix. Released version 1.0.Play the current game → The release test found nothing left to fix.
CP-GOct 29 Timing, surfaces, speed, HARD against SOFT and the jingles were all right. The miss whoosh was too quiet and the starting volume needed to come down. Asked for menu music. A6b and S14b: a louder whoosh, Effects level 5 by default, shoe squeaks, and real dive and menu sounds. Kept the iPhone silent switch muting the game. Menu music was planned, then put on hold on Nov 3.Play the current game → Music is on hold to decide later whether the game wants it at all.
